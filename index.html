Research Focus Areas

Aligned with the Oxford Global Cities Index framework, our research evaluates Jacksonville across five critical dimensions to benchmark our progress toward becoming a world-class city.

Economics

Analyzing GDP growth, economic diversity, employment trends, and per capita income to assess Jacksonville's economic vitality and potential for sustained prosperity.

Human Capital

Examining educational attainment, university presence, corporate headquarters, demographic trends, and talent attraction to evaluate Jacksonville's workforce competitiveness.

Quality of Life

Researching life expectancy, income levels, housing affordability, cultural amenities, and infrastructure to measure the wellbeing and satisfaction of Jacksonville residents.

Environment

Studying air quality, emissions intensity, climate resilience, and natural disaster preparedness to ensure Jacksonville's environmental sustainability and livability.

Governance

Evaluating institutional strength, political stability, business environment, and civil liberties to assess the foundation for Jacksonville's long-term prosperity.
